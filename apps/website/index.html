One system, the whole floor

Every module a real restaurant needs, built in from day one — not add-ons behind a paywall.

01 Floor

Tables & Kitchen, in sync

See every table's live status — occupied, food preparing, ready to serve, payment pending — and bill or clear a table right from its tile. Kitchen tickets move New → Accepted → Preparing → Ready automatically, with a chime for new orders. A repeat order never resends food that's already been served.

02 Orders

Order Workspace

A fast menu-browsing cart for waiters. One table visit is always one running bill, even when the customer orders more after their first course.

03 Payments

Cash, card, UPI, or a link

Demo Mode out of the box — plug in Razorpay any time for live payments.

04 Reports

Know what's working

Revenue trends, top-selling items, waiter performance, table utilization.

05 Staff

Roles, not one shared login

Waiters, cashiers, managers, and admins each get their own login and see exactly what their role needs.

06 Onboarding

Guided setup wizard

Restaurant details, tax rates, your admin login, table count, an optional starter menu — live in under two minutes.

07 Training

Live, spotlighted product tour

New staff get a guided walkthrough of every screen the first time they sign in — replayable any time from Settings.

No cloud, on purpose

Runs entirely on your own Wi-Fi

Install Saffra on one computer in the restaurant — a back-office PC, a POS terminal, anything. It runs its own local server and database right there. Every other device just opens a browser.

✓Every waiter's phone, tablet, or the billing counter connects over the same Wi-Fi — nothing to install on their end.
✓No internet outage ever stops you from taking an order or printing a receipt.
✓Your data — orders, payments, customers — never leaves the building unless you choose to back it up.
